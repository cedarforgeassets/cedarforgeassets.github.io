About

Cedarforge Assets is an independent asset studio from Lebanon, named after the cedar. We build the kind of packs we want in our own games: focused on one theme, consistent in style, and easy to drop into a project.

How we make them: our assets are created with the help of AI tools, then selected, edited, cleaned up and packaged by hand. Every listing says so clearly.
